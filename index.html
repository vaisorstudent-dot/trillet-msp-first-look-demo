Experience The Solution Yourself

01 · Answer

Picks up every inbound call

Trillet answers your service desk line 24/7 — no hold music, no IVR maze. The caller is greeted immediately by a live-sounding AI agent trained on your MSP’s workflows.

02 · Verify

Confirms who’s calling before acting

The agent matches the caller to a known contact and runs your identity verification policy — blocking social engineering at the front door before any action is taken.

03 · Understand

Understands the request in plain speech

No menu selection. The caller describes their issue naturally and Trillet classifies it against your defined scope — determining whether it can be resolved or must escalate.

04 · Resolve or Escalate

Acts inside your stack — or hands off cleanly

For in-scope requests, Trillet executes directly via your integrations (Entra ID, ConnectWise, etc.). Out-of-scope calls are escalated with full context — never dropped.

05 · Ticket & Audit

Every call logged, structured, and auditable

A structured ticket is filed in your PSA automatically. The full call record — what was asked, verified, and done — is stored for compliance and quality review.
