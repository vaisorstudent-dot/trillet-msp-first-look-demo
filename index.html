The bounded workflow

Watch a reset request go the distance.

The target first workflow is deliberately bounded and measurable: the verified password reset request — high-volume, trust-sensitive, bounded, and measurable.

First-look prototype framing. Production actions, PSA write-back, identity checks, and reset execution depend on scoped integrations and approved policy.
01 Verify

Identity before action.

The caller is verified against the MSP’s policy before anything is confirmed or done — closing the help-desk social-engineering surface.

02 Scope

Bounded to policy.

The request is confirmed and scoped to exactly what policy allows — a password reset, nothing else.

03 Act

Executed within scope.

Where live integration is verified, the action path runs through the approved channel — otherwise it falls back to a technician with full context.

04 Follow-through

Recorded and audited.

The service record is prepared with transcript, status, and reason trail. Production PSA write-back depends on integration scope.

Escalation is a first-class outcome. Any break in policy, verification, or confidence routes the call to a human with full context — by design, not as a failure mode.
